Lesson 4 · 15 min

First garden: personal life

Most people use AI as a question machine: open it, ask, copy, close. It's like hiring a brilliant consultant and wiping their memory every morning. In personal life, cultivation is cheap: three or four text files and twenty minutes a week.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Write a one-page About me
  2. 2Pick one function at a time
  3. 3The 20-minute weekly review
  4. 4The 30-day plan

Goal: start personal cultivation with one function, an About me file, three rules and the 20-minute weekly review.

01 · Personal cultivation

Three files and one habit. Click each part.

The week's correction becomes a rule or context, and next week starts from there
Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Someone who does the review for eight weeks ends up with an AI that doesn't look like anyone else's.

02 · Five gardens that pay off fast

Pick one to start.

1. Decisions

Role: an advisor who doesn't decide. Context: your criteria and past decisions with their outcomes. Rule: always present the opposing option.

Over time, it remembers your own patterns: "last time you picked the cheapest and regretted it".

2. Money

Role: spending analyst. Tool: a spreadsheet exported from your bank. Rule: never move money, only show it.

Spending categories and patterns show up that you had never seen.

3. Study

Role: tutor. Examples: one explanation that "clicked" and one that didn't. Assessment: it asks, you answer, it notes where you get stuck.

The memory becomes the map of your gaps.

4. Writing

Role: an editor with your voice. Examples: five of your texts. Rule: don't change the tone, only clarity.

After a month, the first draft comes out almost ready. Health and routine follow the same logic: suggest and ask your doctor, never prescribe.

03 · Templates for your folder

Copy into SOBRE-MIM.md, REGRAS.md, and the review note.

SOBRE-MIM.md
# About me — <name>          reviewed on <date>

Who I am: 
This year's goals: 1)  2)  3) 
Constraints (time, money, health): 
How I like to work: direct, no praise, English, short lists
What I hate: 
Decisions already made (don't reopen):
REGRAS.md
# Rules — role: <e.g.: writing reviewer>

1. Never decide for me on money or health; present options.
2. Be direct, no praise.
3. Answer in English.

# Never
- third-party data or passwords in this file
Weekly review (20 min)
## Review — week of <date>
1. I read MEMORIA.md: what did it note that's new?
2. Three lines in FALHAS.md: date | what went wrong | smallest fix | was context, a rule, or an example missing?
3. I fixed it IN THE FILE (not in the conversation): 
4. I deleted from memory what is no longer true:

04 · 30-day plan

One week per tab.

1. Week 1

Write About me (one page). Pick a role. Set three rules.

2. Week 2

Use it every day in the same role. Gather five good examples. Start the failure log.

3. Week 3

First real weekly review. Edit the context and rules. Create the Memory file.

4. Week 4

Add one tool (calendar or spreadsheet). Measure: time saved and how many times you redid things. Decide whether to open a second role.

05 · Practice

This week.

1 · About me and three rules

25 min

Write SOBRE-MIM.md and REGRAS.md with a single role. The personal kit has the files with a fictional example.

Done when: About me fits on one page, the role is written in one sentence, and there are exactly three rules.

2 · The first weekly review

20 min

Use the AI in the same role for one week, loading the files at the start. At the end, do the review using the template above.

Done when: FALHAS.md has at least three lines, and at least one of them turned into a change in a file (rule, context, or example).
